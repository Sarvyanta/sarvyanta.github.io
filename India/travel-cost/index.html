INDIA TRAVEL GUIDE

How Much Does It Cost to Travel in India?

India can be affordable, comfortable or luxurious depending on how you travel. This guide breaks down the major expenses so you can build a realistic travel budget before your trip.

See Travel Budgets Explore Costs
QUICK VIEW

Your India trip budget depends mainly on:

  • Where you travel
  • How you travel between cities
  • Your accommodation level
  • How often you eat out
  • The experiences you choose
THE SHORT ANSWER

India does not have one single travel price

A traveller can experience India on a relatively modest daily budget, while another traveller may spend considerably more on hotels, private transport, restaurants and experiences.

The most useful way to plan an India trip is therefore not to look for one number. Instead, estimate the major categories separately and then combine them.

Important: The figures below are planning ranges, not fixed prices. Actual costs vary by city, season, booking time, property, transport type and personal preferences.
MAJOR EXPENSES

Where does your travel money go?

Most India trips can be planned around six major spending categories.

🏨

Accommodation

Hotels, hostels, guesthouses, homestays and premium properties can have very different prices.

🍛

Food

Local restaurants, street food, cafés and premium dining create very different daily food budgets.

🚆

Intercity Travel

Trains, buses, domestic flights and private cars provide different combinations of cost, comfort and travel time.

🚕

Local Transport

Metro systems, buses, auto-rickshaws, taxis and ride-hailing services can all be useful depending on the city.

🎟️

Sightseeing

Some public places are inexpensive while major attractions, guided tours and experiences can add to your budget.

🛍️

Shopping & Extras

Souvenirs, clothing, handicrafts, SIM cards and unexpected expenses should have a small budget of their own.

CHOOSE YOUR STYLE

Three ways to think about your India budget

BUDGET

Simple & economical

Suitable for travellers who are comfortable using economical accommodation, local food and public transport.

  • Hostels or simple stays
  • Local restaurants
  • Trains and buses
  • Public transport
  • Low-cost attractions
MID-RANGE

Comfortable travel

A balanced approach for travellers who want comfortable accommodation while still keeping an eye on expenses.

  • Comfortable hotels
  • Mix of local & restaurant dining
  • Trains and selected flights
  • Ride-hailing or taxis when useful
  • Paid experiences
PREMIUM

More comfort & convenience

Designed for travellers who place greater importance on premium accommodation, private transport and experiences.

  • Premium hotels
  • Restaurants and curated dining
  • More domestic flights
  • Private cars or transfers
  • Guided experiences
SAMPLE PLANNING

Think about your trip in 7, 14 or 30 days

Instead of relying on one headline number, build the trip from your expected daily spending plus longer-distance travel.

07

7-Day Trip

A week works well for one region or a small number of nearby destinations.

  • Accommodation × 7 nights
  • Food × 7 days
  • Local transport
  • Intercity transport
  • Sightseeing
14

14-Day Trip

Two weeks gives you more flexibility to combine multiple destinations without rushing every day.

  • Accommodation × 14 nights
  • Food × 14 days
  • Several intercity journeys
  • Local transport
  • More experiences
30

30-Day Trip

A month-long trip makes daily spending discipline more important because small expenses accumulate quickly.

  • Accommodation × 30 nights
  • Food × 30 days
  • Multiple regions
  • Transport between destinations
  • Flexible activity budget
DESTINATION MATTERS

India can feel very different from city to city

Accommodation and lifestyle costs can vary substantially between destinations. Tourist demand, business activity, seasonality and local infrastructure all influence prices.

Mumbai

Major metropolitan destination with high demand for accommodation and significant variation between areas.

Delhi

A major gateway for northern India with a broad range of accommodation, food and transport options.

Bengaluru

Technology and business centre with strong demand for accommodation and services.

Hyderabad

Large technology and business hub with a wide range of accommodation and dining choices.

Goa

Tourism-driven destination where prices can change considerably depending on location and season.

Jaipur

Popular heritage destination offering options ranging from economical stays to heritage and luxury hotels.

TRAVEL SMARTER

Ways to control your India travel budget

01

Travel in the right season

Popular destinations can become more expensive during peak travel periods.

02

Compare transport options

A train, bus or flight may each make sense depending on distance, timing and your priorities.

03

Stay near transport

A slightly different hotel location can sometimes reduce repeated local transport costs.

04

Mix local food with restaurants

Trying local food can be part of the experience while also giving your budget more flexibility.

05

Leave room for surprises

Keep part of the budget available for changes, extra transport, shopping or unexpected expenses.

06

Do not optimise only for price

Safety, location, reliability and travel time can matter just as much as the cheapest available option.

How travelling in India has changed

THEN

Planning was often offline

Travellers commonly depended on travel agents, guidebooks, printed maps, railway counters, telephone calls and recommendations from people they knew.

NOW

Travel is increasingly digital

Travellers can compare accommodation, use digital maps, book transport online, make digital payments and communicate instantly while travelling.

NEXT

Travel may become more personalised

AI travel assistants, personalised itineraries, smarter recommendations and increasingly connected travel services could make planning more adaptive.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

India travel cost questions

Is India cheap to travel?

India can offer economical travel options, but the overall cost depends heavily on accommodation, transport, destination, season and travel style.

How much money should I budget per day?

There is no single useful daily figure for every traveller. A better approach is to estimate accommodation, food, local transport and activities separately.

Is public transport available in India?

Yes. India has extensive rail and bus networks, while many cities also have metro systems and app-based transport services.

Can I use digital payments in India?

Digital payments are widely used in India, although travellers should still maintain a backup payment method and some cash for situations where digital payment is not available or convenient.

Is India suitable for a long trip?

India is large and diverse, so longer trips can allow travellers to explore regions more slowly instead of spending most of their time moving between destinations.
