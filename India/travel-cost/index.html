How Much Does It Cost to Travel in India?

Planning a 7-day, 14-day or 30-day trip to India? Here is a practical way to estimate your spending before you arrive.

Important: These figures cover your spending inside India. Your flight or other travel from your home country to India is not included.
2026 planning range ₹1,500 – ₹25,000+

Approximate daily spending per person, depending on travel style.

WHAT YOUR MONEY COVERS

Where does the daily budget go?

01

Accommodation

Hostel beds, guesthouses, budget hotels, comfortable hotels, boutique properties or luxury hotels.

02

Food

Local meals can be inexpensive, while restaurants, international cuisine and fine dining can increase the daily budget.

03

Local transport

Metro, buses, autos, taxis, app-based cabs and occasional private vehicles.

04

Sightseeing

Museums, monuments, guided tours, activities, attractions and experiences.

05

Small extras

SIM/data, snacks, bottled drinks, tips, shopping and unexpected little expenses.

06

Intercity travel

Trains, buses or domestic flights between destinations. Moving frequently can change the total dramatically.

Hyderabad

Hyderabad can work well for travellers who want a mix of historic attractions, modern city life and local food.

Stay Budget hotels through premium properties are available across different neighbourhoods.
Food Local meals can be inexpensive, while restaurants and hotel dining can increase the budget.
Transport Metro, buses, autos and app-based cabs provide several choices.
Watch for Long distances between attractions can increase cab spending.

Chennai

Chennai offers a combination of South Indian food, temples, beaches, cultural attractions and urban transport.

Stay Central and business districts can have different hotel pricing.
Food Local breakfast and meals can be very budget-friendly.
Transport Metro and public transport options can reduce local travel costs.
Watch for Airport transfers and frequent app-based cabs can add up.

Mumbai

Mumbai can be more expensive for accommodation, especially in popular central areas. Public transport can help control the budget.

Stay Hotel location can make a major difference to the daily budget.
Food From inexpensive local food to high-end dining, the range is huge.
Transport Local trains, metro, buses and app-based cabs offer different price points.
Watch for Premium hotels and frequent private cabs can quickly increase costs.

Delhi

Delhi gives visitors a wide range of accommodation and food choices. The metro can be particularly useful for sightseeing.

Stay Accommodation varies considerably by neighbourhood.
Food Street food, local restaurants and premium restaurants cover a wide range.
Transport The metro can be a low-cost way to move around many parts of the city.
Watch for Airport transfers and private sightseeing vehicles can increase the budget.

Goa

Goa is one of the clearest examples of why season matters. Accommodation and transport can change considerably during popular holiday periods.

Food Local eateries and beach restaurants offer very different price points.

Jaipur

THE UNOFFICIAL INDIA TRAVEL RULE 😄

And then there is the famous... “Tourist Price”

India does not have an official “foreigner price” for everyday things. But visitors may sometimes encounter higher quotes in tourist-heavy areas, markets or informal negotiations.

Think of it as the moment when a ₹100 question suddenly becomes a ₹300 conversation. 😄

Ask first Confirm the fare before starting a ride or service.
Compare Check another shop, driver or app when practical.
Use apps App-based transport can make pricing clearer.
Keep perspective Most people you meet are simply trying to do business.

In short: negotiate politely, know the approximate local price, and don't let one awkward quote become your definition of India.

📅

About these 2026 figures

These are planning estimates based on 2026 information and observed price ranges. Actual costs can be higher or lower depending on the city, season, exchange rates, hotel availability, transport choice, attractions and your personal spending style.

Planning for 2027? Prices may change slightly — or sometimes significantly in a particular city or season. Treat these numbers as a starting point rather than a guaranteed quotation.

If you have a larger budget, you can choose better hotels, private transport and premium experiences. If your budget is smaller, local food, public transport, hostels and slower travel can bring the cost down considerably.

MAKE IT YOUR TRIP

Your budget is a choice, not a rule.

You do not have to travel at one fixed level for the entire trip.

🎒

Save where it doesn't matter

Use public transport for some journeys and spend more on an experience you really care about.

FAQ

Common questions

Does this include my flight to India?

No. International travel to and from India is deliberately excluded. These figures are for spending inside India.

Are these prices per person?

Yes. The headline daily and trip ranges are intended as per-person planning figures. Hotel rooms and private transport can sometimes be shared, which may reduce the per-person cost for couples or groups.

Can I travel around India for less?

Yes. Travelling slowly, using hostels or simple guesthouses, eating local food and using public transport can reduce costs.

Can I spend much more than these amounts?

Absolutely. Luxury hotels, private drivers, premium trains, domestic flights, private guides and high-end dining can push daily spending far beyond the planning ranges shown here.

Why can two people get completely different prices?

Because India is not one price market. City, season, hotel, transport, sightseeing and personal preferences can all change the final bill.
