One country.
Many stories.

India is constantly changing. The way people learn, travel, work, shop, communicate, eat and manage money has transformed across generations.

Sarvyanta India Explained looks at those changes in a simple way — comparing the past with the present and exploring possible directions for the future.

Historical facts, current observations and possible future developments are clearly distinguished.
