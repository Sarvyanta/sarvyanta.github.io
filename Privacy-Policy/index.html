Privacy Policy

Sarvyanta respects the privacy of visitors and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and managed when you use Sarvyanta websites and services.

This policy is intended to cover Sarvyanta's websites, informational content, educational resources, tools, calculators, enquiry services, local discovery and lead-generation projects, and other services that link to this policy.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to Sarvyanta web pages and services that link to it, including relevant current and future projects operated under the Sarvyanta name.

These may include:

  • Parenting, family, health-information and New-Mom resources.
  • India — Then, Now & Next and cultural or informational content.
  • Tuition Connect, learning resources and educational tools.
  • Local business discovery, service directories and enquiry services.
  • Calculators, guided helpers and small-business tools.
  • Business websites, demonstrations and digital services.
  • Other informational, community or digital projects published by Sarvyanta.

A specific service may provide an additional privacy notice where it collects information or has requirements that are not adequately described by this general policy. If a separate notice applies, please read it alongside this policy.

External websites and third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Information That May Be Processed

The information involved depends on which pages and features you use. It may include:

  • Technical and usage information: pages visited, referring pages, browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical information, and interaction with website features.
  • Enquiry information: name, email address, phone number, class or subject preferences, general service location, and the details you choose to include when contacting us.
  • Service information: information you submit to use a calculator, learning resource, local service or other feature, where applicable.
  • Communication information: the contents of messages you send to Sarvyanta and information needed to respond to you.
  • Preference information: language selection and other preferences that a website feature may need to function.

Some pages can be viewed without submitting information directly to Sarvyanta. However, hosting, analytics, security, advertising or other third-party technologies may process technical information when those features are active.

Please do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, financial account details, or other sensitive information unless a particular service explicitly requires it through an appropriate and secure process.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure Sarvyanta websites and services.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • Connect people with relevant tutors, businesses or service providers.
  • Improve content, navigation, accessibility and mobile usability.
  • Understand general traffic patterns and feature usage.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, fraud and technical problems.
  • Maintain records needed to manage an enquiry or service.
  • Measure the performance of content, outreach and promotions.
  • Display and measure advertising on pages where advertising is enabled.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Sarvyanta aims to use information for purposes relevant to the feature or service through which it was provided.

4. Google Analytics and Website Measurement

Sarvyanta may use Google Analytics and similar measurement technologies to understand how visitors use its websites and improve the user experience.

Depending on the configuration and features in use, these technologies may process page views, browser and device details, approximate geographic information, traffic sources and interactions with website elements.

Website events may measure actions such as selecting a category, opening a resource or clicking an enquiry button. Such events should use general event information rather than directly identifying details.

Sarvyanta should not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp message contents, children's identifying information, or other directly identifying personal information as Google Analytics event parameters.

Analytics data may be processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy policies. Analytics configuration, retention settings and consent controls may vary by service.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Sarvyanta websites and third-party services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, scripts or similar technologies where needed for functionality, security, analytics or advertising.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember settings or preferences where implemented.
  • Measure website usage and performance.
  • Help detect abuse or technical issues.
  • Support advertising and advertising measurement where enabled.

You may be able to manage or delete cookies through your browser. Blocking some technologies may affect certain features. Browser controls do not necessarily disable every form of measurement or processing.

Where consent is legally required for a technology, the relevant consent mechanism should be implemented before that technology is activated.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Advertising Services

Some Sarvyanta websites may display third-party advertisements or use advertising services, including Google AdSense, if enabled and approved for the relevant site.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, limit, personalise where permitted, measure or improve advertisements. The information processed depends on the provider, the configuration and the choices available to the visitor.

Sarvyanta does not control all data-processing practices of third-party advertising providers. Their own policies and settings also apply.

For information about Google's use of data on partner websites, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

This policy does not mean advertisements are displayed on every Sarvyanta page. Individual pages may not show advertisements.

7. WhatsApp, Email and Other Contact Methods

Sarvyanta pages may provide links to WhatsApp, email, telephone or other communication services so visitors can ask questions or submit enquiries.

If you choose to use an external communication service, the information you provide may be processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sarvyanta may receive the information you choose to send in an enquiry. This information may be used to respond to your request and, where relevant, follow up on the service you asked about.

Clicking a WhatsApp button does not give Sarvyanta access to your WhatsApp contact list or reveal the people or groups to whom you forward a message. Sarvyanta may measure a button click through website analytics if that measurement is configured.

Please review the privacy terms of the communication service before sharing sensitive information.

8. Enquiries, Tutors and Local Service Connections

Some Sarvyanta services help visitors discover tutors, businesses or other service providers, or submit an enquiry about a service.

If you submit an enquiry, information you provide may be used to understand your request, respond to you, check relevant availability and, where appropriate, facilitate a connection with a tutor, business or service provider.

Information will be shared with another party for an enquiry only where relevant to the requested service and subject to applicable requirements. You should be told when a particular service requires information to be passed to a provider.

A provider receiving an enquiry may handle the information under its own privacy practices. Sarvyanta cannot guarantee a provider's conduct, availability, service quality, pricing or outcome.

Sarvyanta aims to avoid collecting more information than is reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry.

9. Educational Resources and Children's Information

Sarvyanta may publish educational materials, worksheets, tutoring resources, parenting information and content intended for parents, caregivers, teachers or learners.

Where a service accepts an enquiry involving a child, a parent or legal guardian should provide only information reasonably necessary for that enquiry. Avoid submitting a child's full identifying details or sensitive information in public messages or general contact forms.

Sarvyanta does not intend general informational pages to require visitors to disclose sensitive personal information about children. If a specific service is designed to collect information directly from children, it requires an appropriate review of privacy, consent and applicable legal requirements before launch.

10. Health, Parenting and Informational Content

Sarvyanta may publish general parenting, family, wellness or health-related information. Such content is for general informational and educational purposes and is not a substitute for individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Visitors should not submit medical records or sensitive health information through general website forms or analytics features. Any future service intended to handle such information should have appropriate safeguards and a specific privacy notice.

Reading health or parenting content does not, by itself, require you to provide medical or personal information to Sarvyanta.

11. Calculators, Guided Helpers and Digital Tools

Sarvyanta may provide calculators, document guides, learning helpers, business tools and other interactive features.

Some tools may work entirely in your browser. Others may process information needed to provide a requested function. The details depend on the particular tool and its implementation.

Unless a tool clearly explains otherwise, do not enter passwords, bank account credentials, government identification numbers, private employment records or other sensitive data.

Calculations, guidance and generated information may be estimates or general information. Check important financial, employment, legal, educational or other decisions against the relevant official source or qualified professional.

12. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Sarvyanta does not intend to sell visitors' personal information as a general business practice. Information may be disclosed in limited circumstances, including:

  • To service providers that support hosting, security, analytics, communications, advertising or website operations.
  • To a tutor, business or service provider when needed to address an enquiry or provide a requested connection, subject to the relevant notice and applicable requirements.
  • When you request, authorise or otherwise consent to a disclosure.
  • When required by applicable law, a valid legal process or a lawful request from an authorised authority.
  • When reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, maintain security or protect legal rights.

Third-party providers may process information under their own terms. Sarvyanta aims to use appropriate providers and limit disclosures to what is relevant to the purpose.

13. External Websites and Links

Sarvyanta pages may link to external websites, official resources, social media platforms, communication services or third-party tools.

Sarvyanta does not control the privacy, security, content or practices of those external services. Visiting an external link may subject you to that provider's policies and terms.

Review the applicable policy before submitting personal information to an external website.

14. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide a requested service, maintain appropriate records, address disputes, preserve security or meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, and the provider processing it. Analytics and third-party services may have their own retention settings.

Information that is no longer reasonably needed should be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled appropriately, subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations.

15. Security

Sarvyanta aims to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled through its websites and services.

No website, storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should use care when sharing information online and avoid submitting sensitive information through general enquiry channels.

16. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Depending on the service, applicable law and the information involved, you may contact Sarvyanta to:

  • Ask what personal information you submitted through a Sarvyanta service.
  • Request correction or deletion of information you provided.
  • Ask a question about how information is used or shared.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is available.
  • Raise a concern about a privacy practice.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant enquiry before acting on a request. We may also need to retain certain information where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

You can also use browser controls to manage some cookies and similar technologies. Certain third-party services provide their own privacy and advertising preference controls.

Requests will be considered under the laws and requirements applicable to the relevant service and circumstances.

17. International Visitors and Processing

Sarvyanta content and services may be accessible to visitors in India and other countries. Hosting, analytics, communications or other service providers may process information in locations different from the visitor's location.

Where applicable, information transfers and processing are subject to relevant legal requirements and the terms of the providers involved.

Visitors using a service from outside India should also consider any additional notice or requirements applicable to that particular service.

18. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Sarvyanta aims to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements. The requirements that apply may depend on the service, the information involved and the visitor's location.

Where applicable, a service may need to provide additional information about the purpose of processing, consent, retention, data sharing or available rights.

This policy is general information about Sarvyanta's intended practices. It does not replace service-specific notices or create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sarvyanta may update this policy when its websites, features, third-party services, business practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Visitors should review it periodically. If a material change requires additional notice or consent, the relevant steps should be taken where applicable.
