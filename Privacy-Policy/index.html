Privacy Policy

Sarvyanta respects your privacy and is committed to handling website visitors' information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit Sarvyanta websites and pages that link to this policy.

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is intended to apply across Sarvyanta websites, pages, services and informational content that link to this policy.

Individual Sarvyanta services may have additional information or enquiry requirements. Where applicable, those requirements may be explained on the relevant page.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use a Sarvyanta website, limited information may be processed through website technologies and third-party services.

This may include information such as:

  • pages visited and general website usage information;
  • approximate technical information associated with a website visit;
  • browser or device-related information made available through normal web technologies;
  • information voluntarily provided when a visitor sends an enquiry or contacts Sarvyanta.

Sarvyanta does not intentionally request or collect sensitive personal information merely for browsing informational pages.

3. Google Analytics

Sarvyanta may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our websites and to improve content, navigation and services.

Analytics may process technical and usage information associated with visits to our pages.

Sarvyanta does not intentionally send names, phone numbers, WhatsApp contact lists or other directly identifying personal information to Google Analytics as analytics event parameters.

4. WhatsApp Links and Sharing

Some Sarvyanta pages may provide buttons allowing you to contact or share content through WhatsApp.

When you choose to use WhatsApp, you are interacting with WhatsApp and its services. WhatsApp's own privacy practices and terms apply to that interaction.

Sarvyanta may measure that a website visitor selected a WhatsApp share or enquiry button. This is used to understand engagement and improve the website.

Sarvyanta does not use the WhatsApp share button to obtain your WhatsApp contact list or identify the people or groups to whom you forward a page.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

Some Sarvyanta pages may display advertisements using Google AdSense.

Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertising.

Advertising and related technologies may process information according to the policies of the relevant third-party provider.

Visitors should also review Google's policies and information regarding advertising technologies where appropriate.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Sarvyanta websites and third-party services used on those websites may use cookies or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as analytics, website functionality, security and advertising.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Some website functionality or advertising features may be affected by those choices.

7. Enquiries and Voluntary Information

If you voluntarily contact Sarvyanta through an enquiry form, WhatsApp, email or another communication method, information you provide may be used to respond to your request.

Please avoid sending passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information through website enquiry channels.

8. Children's Privacy

Sarvyanta aims to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information from children.

Informational pages may contain general educational or cultural content intended for families and the public. Visitors should not submit personal information about children unless it is necessary and appropriate for the particular service.

9. Third-Party Services

Sarvyanta may use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, hosting, communication and website infrastructure providers.

Those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Security

Sarvyanta takes reasonable steps to protect information handled through its websites and services.

However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

Sarvyanta may update this Privacy Policy when its websites, services, analytics, advertising or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have a privacy-related question about a Sarvyanta website or service, please contact Sarvyanta through the contact method provided on the relevant Sarvyanta website or service.
