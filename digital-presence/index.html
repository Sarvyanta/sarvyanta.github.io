THE BASIC OFFER

What do selected partners receive?

We provide a basic business webpage at no charge to selected partners, within the features and scope described below.

01

A dedicated business webpage

Your own business page under a Sarvyanta web address, such as yourbusiness.sarvyanta.com.

02

Business introduction

Tell customers who you are, what your business does, where you operate and what makes your offering useful.

03

Products and services

Showcase your products, services, descriptions and available information so visitors can understand what you offer.

04

Photos and work gallery

Display suitable product photographs, completed work, creations or other images relevant to your business.

05

Direct customer contact

Include your contact details and a WhatsApp enquiry option so interested customers can reach you directly.

06

Mobile-friendly presentation

Your page will be designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

The free offering covers the standard features listed above. The final page content and scope will be agreed with each selected business.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

A few things to know.

Free for selected partners

The standard business page is offered free of charge to businesses selected by Sarvyanta. This is not an automatic or universal offer.

Custom domains and additional costs

The standard offer uses a Sarvyanta subdomain. If you prefer your own domain, domain registration and renewal costs are separate. Custom features, advanced integrations, additional functionality, extensive content work or services outside the standard offering may involve additional charges. Any such costs will be discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

Content and updates

The business is responsible for providing accurate information, photographs and necessary permissions for submitted content. Reasonable corrections to the agreed page content can be discussed with Sarvyanta. Major changes or additional work may fall outside the free offering.

Promotions and visibility

Inclusion in promotional activities depends on availability, suitability and Sarvyanta's discretion. Promotion does not guarantee website traffic, search rankings, customer enquiries or sales.

Sarvyanta Market listings

Not every partner product will automatically appear in Sarvyanta Market. Product inclusion is subject to review, eligibility, available space and any applicable listing or participation conditions.

Business ownership and transactions

Participating businesses remain responsible for their products, services, pricing, fulfilment, customer communication, warranties and applicable legal requirements. Unless separately agreed, customers contact and transact directly with the business.
