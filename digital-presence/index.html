A BIGGER PICTURE

Not just an online page. A place for your business to grow.

A website gives your business a place on the internet. But building a useful digital presence can go further than that. It is about presenting what you do, helping people discover your offerings and creating opportunities to connect.

Sarvyanta aims to bring independent businesses, creators, products and customers closer together. We start by understanding your business and creating a suitable digital identity. From there, we can explore relevant promotional opportunities and connections through Sarvyanta Market.

The intention is to build a useful, transparent relationship rather than simply create a website and walk away.

OUR STANDARD PARTNER OFFERING

A digital starting point. At no cost for selected partners.

Sarvyanta intends to make it easier for selected independent businesses to establish their digital identity. The standard digital presence offering is provided free of cost to selected partners.

This gives a business an opportunity to establish its own online space without having to begin with a custom-domain website or a complex digital setup.

STANDARD OFFERING
Free for selected partners

The standard offering may include:

  • Business page on a Sarvyanta subdomain
  • Business introduction and contact information
  • Services or product presentation
  • Images and gallery, where relevant
  • WhatsApp enquiry link
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • Shareable website address

The exact scope and included features will be agreed with each selected partner before the work begins.

+

Custom domains and additional features

A separately registered domain, domain renewals, advanced functionality, extra pages, integrations or requirements beyond the standard scope may involve additional charges. These will be discussed and agreed upon beforehand.
