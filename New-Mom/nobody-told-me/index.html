Honest conversations about motherhood

Nobody Told Me About Motherhood

You can love your baby deeply and still find motherhood difficult. You can feel grateful and overwhelmed, happy and exhausted, confident one moment and uncertain the next.

This is a space for the thoughts and feelings mothers sometimes keep to themselves because they worry nobody else will understand.

Explore the stories

You are allowed to talk about the difficult parts

Motherhood does not have to feel wonderful every minute for your love for your child to be real. Difficult feelings do not make you a bad mother. You deserve kindness, understanding, and support too.

A gentle reminder

You do not have to be a perfect mother. You are a person learning something new, often while tired, healing, and caring for someone who depends on you.

Your feelings matter. Your rest matters. Your well-being matters.
