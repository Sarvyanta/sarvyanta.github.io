Nobody Told Me About This Part of Motherhood

Everyone talks about the baby, the milestones, and the beautiful moments. But there are parts of becoming a mother that can be hard to explain. This is a space for those feelings, too.

You can be grateful for your baby and still find motherhood difficult. You can love this new life and miss parts of your old one. Both feelings can exist together.

A little reassurance

You do not have to figure everything out alone

There is no single way to feel after becoming a mother. Give yourself permission to talk about the difficult days, ask for help, and seek professional support when you need it.
