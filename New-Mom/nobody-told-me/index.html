The honest side of motherhood

Nobody Told Me About This Part

Motherhood can bring moments of extraordinary love, but it can also bring feelings you did not expect. You may miss your old life, feel overwhelmed, worry about your baby or wonder why nobody asks how you are doing. These experiences deserve understanding, not judgement.

You can love your baby deeply and still find motherhood difficult. Both feelings can be true, and you deserve support through both.

For the mother behind the smiles

You do not have to pretend everything is easy

You may feel pressure to be grateful, patient, organised and happy because you have become a mother. You may compare your experience with photographs, social media posts or stories from other families.

But what you see from the outside is rarely the whole story. Every family has different circumstances, and every mother has her own physical, emotional and practical needs.

If you are struggling, you do not need to wait until things become unbearable before talking to someone. A trusted person, a healthcare professional or a qualified mental health professional may help you understand what you are experiencing and find appropriate support.

A gentle reminder: These articles offer general information and emotional support, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional healthcare. If distress is persistent or severe, seek professional help. If you or your baby are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Remember this

  • You can love your baby and still need a break.
  • Asking for help is not a failure.
  • Your physical and emotional recovery matters.
  • You do not need to compare your journey with another mother's.
  • You deserve to be listened to without judgement.
