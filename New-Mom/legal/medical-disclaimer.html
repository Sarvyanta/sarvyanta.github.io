Your health matters

Medical Disclaimer

Information on pregnancy, birth, and motherhood can offer support, but it cannot replace care tailored to you and your baby.

Please remember: Sarvyanta New-Mom is an informational website. Its content is not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

1. General information only

Articles on this website discuss pregnancy, labour and birth, breastfeeding, postpartum recovery, emotional wellbeing, and motherhood. They are intended to provide general education, encouragement, and information for readers.

The content is not a substitute for a consultation, examination, diagnosis, or treatment by a qualified doctor, midwife, mental health professional, lactation consultant, or other appropriate healthcare provider.

2. Every pregnancy and family is different

Pregnancy, childbirth, recovery, feeding, and infant development vary from person to person. An experience described in an article may not match yours, and information that is appropriate in one situation may not be suitable in another.

Do not start, stop, or change medication, supplements, treatment, feeding practices, or medical care solely because of information on this website. Discuss personal decisions with your healthcare professional.

3. Pregnancy and childbirth

Information about pregnancy symptoms, baby movements, labour, delivery, and recovery is general in nature. It cannot determine whether a symptom is normal or whether you or your baby need medical attention.

Contact your maternity care team promptly if you are worried about your health, your pregnancy, your baby's movements, bleeding, severe pain, fluid leakage, or any new or worsening symptom. Follow the specific instructions given by your own care team.

4. Breastfeeding and infant care

Breastfeeding and infant-feeding articles provide general information and do not replace individualized advice about milk supply, latch, feeding frequency, medication while breastfeeding, infant weight gain, or your baby's health.

Seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional if your baby is feeding poorly, has fewer wet nappies than expected, seems unusually sleepy, has breathing difficulties, or shows other concerning symptoms. For urgent or severe symptoms, seek emergency medical care.

5. Postpartum and mental health

Articles about baby blues, anxiety, postpartum depression, loneliness, identity, and mom guilt are intended to support understanding and reduce isolation. They cannot diagnose a mental health condition or establish whether a particular treatment is right for you.

If emotional distress persists, feels overwhelming, or affects your ability to function or care for yourself, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare or mental health professional. You deserve support, and asking for help is appropriate.

If you may be in immediate danger

If you or someone else is at immediate risk of harm, or you have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, seek urgent help from local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, contact a trusted person who can stay with you while help is arranged.

6. Accuracy and updates

We aim to make content clear and helpful, but medical knowledge, recommendations, and clinical guidance may change. Information may become outdated or may not cover every relevant circumstance. We do not guarantee that every article is complete, current, or applicable to every reader.

Always confirm important health decisions with a qualified professional who can consider your medical history and current needs.

7. Personal responsibility

You are responsible for decisions you make after reading this website. Sarvyanta New-Mom does not provide individual medical care through its articles, and reading or sharing website content does not create a healthcare professional-patient relationship.

8. External resources

We may link to other websites or resources for additional reading. We do not control all external content and cannot guarantee its accuracy, availability, or suitability. Check the source and seek professional guidance before relying on health information from an external website.

9. Contact and emergencies

Questions about website content may be directed through an official Sarvyanta contact channel, if available. These channels are not intended for diagnosis, emergency support, or time-critical medical questions.

If you are concerned about your health or your baby's health, contact your healthcare professional directly rather than waiting for a response from a website.
