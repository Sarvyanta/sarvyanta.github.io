A little preparation

Your Hospital Bag Checklist

You do not need to pack your entire home. Start with the essentials, check your hospital's instructions and leave room for the unexpected.

“Preparation can help you feel more settled, even when you cannot plan every part of birth.”

Documents and important information

  • Hospital registration details and identification documents requested by the hospital.
  • Pregnancy records, scan reports and test results, as advised by your care team.
  • Current medication list and relevant allergy information.
  • Insurance or payment information, if needed.
  • Emergency contact details.

For the mother

  • Comfortable, loose clothing and a change of clothes for going home.
  • Nightwear and underwear that suit your needs.
  • Maternity pads, if the hospital asks you to bring them.
  • Toothbrush, toothpaste, soap and other personal toiletries.
  • Slippers or comfortable footwear.
  • Phone, charger and any permitted personal comfort item.
  • Glasses or other everyday essentials you use.
  • Any prescribed medicines in their original packaging, after confirming hospital procedures.

For your baby

  • Newborn clothes suitable for the weather.
  • A few extra outfits in case of spills.
  • Newborn nappies, if not supplied by the hospital.
  • Soft cloths and any baby-care items your hospital recommends.
  • A suitable baby blanket, according to local weather and hospital advice.
  • An appropriate car seat if travelling by car where one is required or recommended.

For your birth partner

  • Identification, phone and charger.
  • Water and simple snacks, following hospital rules.
  • A change of clothes and essential toiletries for a longer stay.
  • Contact details and a note of practical arrangements at home.

Before you zip the bag

Ask your maternity hospital what it supplies and what it does not allow. Some facilities provide baby clothes, nappies or maternity products; others have different requirements. Keep important documents easy to reach and tell your birth partner where they are.

If packing feels overwhelming, make a short checklist and do a few items at a time. A bag that is practical for your circumstances is enough.

Reminder: This is a general checklist, not a hospital-specific list. Follow your maternity unit's instructions and ask your care team about any medical equipment or prescribed items you may need.
