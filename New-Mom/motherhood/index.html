THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

Motherhood: Finding Yourself Along the Way

Becoming a mother changes more than your daily routine. It can reshape your identity, relationships, emotions and understanding of yourself. You are allowed to grow into this new chapter at your own pace.

A SPACE FOR YOU

You are still becoming yourself

Loving your child and missing parts of your old life can exist together. You can feel grateful and overwhelmed, confident one day and uncertain the next. These experiences do not make you any less of a mother.

You do not have to lose yourself to become a good mother. There is room for your child, your changing life and the person you are still discovering.

A GENTLE REMINDER

There is no single right way to be a mother

Your family, circumstances, energy and needs are unique. You can learn from others without measuring your motherhood against theirs.

Take what helps, leave what does not, and give yourself permission to ask for support when things feel difficult.
