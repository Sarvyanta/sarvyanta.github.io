THE FIRST DAYS AND MONTHS

Early Motherhood: Finding Your Feet

Bringing your baby home begins a chapter filled with new experiences, changing routines and emotions you may not have expected. You are learning about your baby while recovering, adjusting and discovering what works for your family. You do not need to have everything figured out at once.

A NEW CHAPTER

You are learning together

The early days can be tender, tiring, joyful, confusing or all of these at once. Your baby is getting used to the world, and you are getting used to caring for someone new while navigating changes in your own body, emotions and daily life.

You do not need to be a perfect mother from the beginning. You and your baby are learning each other, one day at a time.

SUPPORT MATTERS

You deserve care, too

Looking after a newborn can demand a great deal of your time and energy. Your physical recovery, emotional wellbeing, food, rest and support are important parts of this chapter as well.

If you feel persistently low, overwhelmed or anxious, or if you have concerns about your recovery or your baby's health, contact a qualified healthcare professional. You do not need to wait until things become unbearable to ask for help.
