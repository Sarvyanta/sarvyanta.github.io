The first days and weeks

Early Motherhood

Bringing your baby home can bring love, change, uncertainty and exhaustion. Explore gentle, practical reading for the parts of early motherhood that people may not always talk about.

Explore the articles Medical disclaimer

You deserve care, too

The first days with a baby can be beautiful, confusing, tiring and emotional. You may be recovering from birth, learning how to feed your baby, adjusting to broken sleep and discovering feelings you did not expect.

You do not have to understand everything immediately or manage everything by yourself. Support, rest and asking for help are important parts of caring for your family.

You are learning your baby, recovering and adjusting to a new chapter of life. You deserve patience while you do it.

Your wellbeing matters

You do not have to wait until things feel unbearable.

If low mood, anxiety or distress is affecting your daily life, please contact a qualified healthcare professional. You deserve support, and asking for it is a positive step.

If you have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, hallucinations, severe confusion or feel detached from reality, seek urgent medical help.
