THE JOURNEY BEGINS EARLY

Motherhood doesn't begin in a single moment.

For many women, the journey begins with two lines on a pregnancy test — long before the first cry, the first feeding, or the first time she holds her baby.

A first-time mother may experience happiness and fear at the same time. She may imagine the baby she has not yet met, worry about whether everything is going well, prepare for delivery, wonder whether she is ready and slowly begin to imagine herself as someone's mother.

Then the baby arrives — and another journey begins.

A MESSAGE TO EVERY FIRST-TIME MOTHER

You are learning.
Your baby is learning too.

You don't need to have everything figured out. You don't need to be perfect. You are allowed to rest, cry, ask for help, miss your old life and still love your new one.

You are not failing because motherhood feels difficult.
