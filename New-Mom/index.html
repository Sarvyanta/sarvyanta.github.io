Pregnancy changes more than a body. It slowly changes the way a woman sees herself, her future, her relationships and the life she is about to enter.

This is a place for that journey — the excitement, uncertainty, questions, fears, beautiful moments and difficult ones too.

Becoming one moment at a time
MORE THAN BABY CARE

Motherhood begins long before the baby arrives.

A first-time mother is not simply preparing for a baby. She is preparing to become someone she has never been before.

There may be happiness, fear, excitement, confusion, confidence, doubt — sometimes all within the same day.

We want to talk about that woman too.

THE PART PEOPLE DON'T ALWAYS TALK ABOUT

You can love your baby and still find motherhood difficult.

These feelings do not cancel each other out. Happiness and exhaustion can exist together. Gratitude and loneliness can exist together. Love and uncertainty can exist together.

Overwhelmed Excited Lonely Grateful Tired Anxious Proud Confused Changed Hopeful Scared Loved
YOU DON'T HAVE TO KNOW EVERYTHING

Motherhood is not a test you have to pass.

You learn your baby. You learn yourself. You make mistakes. You ask questions. You try again. And slowly, what once felt unfamiliar begins to feel like yours.
