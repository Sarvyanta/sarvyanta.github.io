A note for you

You do not have to feel only happy

People may ask about the baby, appointments and preparations. You deserve space to talk about how you are doing, too.

If worry, sadness or emotional distress feels intense, persists or interferes with daily life, speak with your doctor or maternity care professional. You deserve support throughout pregnancy.
